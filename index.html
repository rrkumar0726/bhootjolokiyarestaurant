Our Story
Born from
Fire & Spice

Named after Assam's legendary ghost pepper — the Bhoot Jolokia — we bring bold Indo-Chinese flavors to the streets of Guwahati. From crispy momos to fiery chowmein, every bite is crafted with heart, heat, and authenticity.

Located on Panjabari Road, Batahguli, we serve dine-in, takeaway, kerbside pickup, and home delivery via Zomato. Quick bites, small plates, and full meals — all made fresh to order.
